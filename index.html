Who I am

I’m a recent BCA graduate from Noble University, Junagadh, currently building my career as a UI/UX-minded Full Stack Developer. My backend foundation is Python, Django and Django REST Framework, paired with PostgreSQL and MySQL for data, and OpenCV when a project needs computer vision.

On the design side, I plan flows, wireframe, and put together interfaces with a consistent visual language before I ever open an editor — which is why my projects tend to look considered rather than assembled.

I’m actively looking for Full Stack or Python/Django Developer roles across Gujarat, and I’m open to remote opportunities across India.
